Enquiries

Place an Order

We'd love to create something special for you!

When placing an enquiry, please include:

  • Your name
  • Contact number
  • Type of product
  • Quantity required
  • Occasion
  • Preferred collection or delivery date
  • Theme or colour scheme
  • Reference pictures (if any)

We'll get back to you with availability, pricing, and any additional information as soon as possible.

Ordering Policy

  • Cakes and large orders must be placed 1–2 weeks in advance.
  • Other sweet treats require 3–5 days' notice.
  • Rush orders may be subject to an additional fee.
  • Cakes and large orders require a 50% non-refundable deposit to confirm the booking.
  • The remaining balance must be paid 3–5 days before collection or delivery.
  • Full payment is required upfront for all other sweet treats.
  • Delivery is available at an additional charge.
  • Please arrive at the agreed collection time.
  • Once an order has been collected or delivered, Sweet Treats by Abigail is not responsible for any damage.
